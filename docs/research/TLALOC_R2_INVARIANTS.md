# Tlaloc R2 invariants

- Tlaloc is the capability foundry/Behavior Lab, not the Tonal runtime.
- Tlaloques are bounded and cannot self-promote.
- Parrot is one probabilistic Tlaloque with no special authority.
- BehaviorSpec + invariants remain behavioral authority.
- Representation ownership remains outside Tlaloc.
- Distillation may target multiple artifact classes, not only prompts.
- Episode recurrence creates candidates, not automatic promotions.
- Promotion requires explicit evidence and declared competence boundaries.
