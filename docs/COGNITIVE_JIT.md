# Cognitive JIT — Tlaloc research direction

## Goal

Cognitive JIT is the hypothesis that repeated verified experience can be converted into cheaper reusable structure, reducing the need to re-solve the same uncertainty with a general model.

Target lifecycle:

```text
novel execution
      ↓
verified Episode
      ↓
recurring reliable structure
      ↓
candidate capability
      ↓
test + holdout + ablation + cost analysis
      ↓
promote or reject
      ↓
reuse through Tonal Registry
```

## Candidate outputs

A compiled result may be a deterministic operation, Machine/state machine, tool wrapper, Shponglese motif/program, prompt/policy, specialized model, probabilistic Tlaloque or hybrid capability.

Do not force every candidate into the same artifact class.

## Promotion is not recurrence

Repeated trajectories are only candidates. Promotion requires evidence that the abstraction preserves useful behavior and justifies its added complexity.

Candidate factors may include:

- reuse frequency;
- held-out reliability;
- verification pass rate;
- failure-root consistency;
- neural/model calls avoided;
- latency/cost reduction;
- representation complexity;
- ablation results.

## Scientific inspirations

DreamCoder and Stitch motivate reusable abstraction/library learning; Voyager motivates skill reuse; Distilling Step-by-Step motivates transferring structured supervision into smaller specialists; MDL motivates balancing explanatory compression against model/library complexity.

These papers motivate mechanisms, not proof that Cognitive JIT works in Tonal.
